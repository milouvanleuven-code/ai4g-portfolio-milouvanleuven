**Project title:**
Studyloop 
**My pair partner:**
Murat Ohran
**Tool we had to use:**

**SDG we had to address:**
SDG 4 - Quality education
**What problem does it solve, and for whom?**
StudyLoop is designed for students who have to learn large amounts of factual information, such as concepts, definitions and terminology. Traditional studying can become inefficient because students often spend the same amount of time on information they already understand as on topics they find difficult. StudyLoop helps students identify what needs more practice and focus their study time on those areas.

**What did you build?**
We developed StudyLoop, an interactive flashcard application that uses spaced repetition. Users can create their own study decks and add flashcards individually, import multiple cards at once, or use AI to generate cards from their study notes. During practice, users rate how well they knew each answer. Based on this feedback, difficult cards appear more frequently while well-known cards are shown less often. This creates a more personalized and efficient learning process.

**Link to the live thing (if any):**
https://lovable.dev/projects/d23b3830-463d-4f94-ab34-4d578b1e9eee?magic_link=mc_4acbf619-f936-4220-9168-3fc030d0fc5d

**How do I run it?**
First, create an account or log in. From the dashboard, create a new study deck and add your flashcards. Cards can be entered manually, added in bulk using the question | answer format, or generated with AI from your notes. Once the deck is ready, start a practice session and rate your knowledge after each card. Your learning progress can then be viewed on the deck page and dashboard.

**Who did what?**
We worked collaboratively throughout the project and divided the workload evenly. Before implementing new ideas or making significant changes, we discussed our options together and decided how we wanted to develop the application.

**Ethical reflection - what are the risks of your tool? Who could it harm?**
One important concern is privacy. When users use the AI feature, the notes they enter may be processed by an external AI service. Users should therefore avoid entering confidential or sensitive information. Another risk is the reliability of AI-generated flashcards. AI can misunderstand the source material or generate incorrect information. If a student does not verify the generated cards, they could unintentionally study incorrect information. For this reason, AI-generated content should support the learning process rather than be treated as automatically correct._
