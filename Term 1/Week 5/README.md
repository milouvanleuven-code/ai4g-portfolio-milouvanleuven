## 2. Hackathon prototype -> [`hackathon/`](hackathon/)

**Project title:**
Plastic vs. Planet
**My pair partner:**
Kadir Guclu
**Tool we had to use:**
ComfyUI
**SDG we had to address:**
SDG 13 — Climate Action
**What problem does it solve, and for whom?**
Plastic packaging creates a huge amount of waste. In the EU, the average person generated 35.3 kg of plastic packaging waste in 2023, of which only 14.8 kg was recycled. Globally, around 430 million tonnes of plastic are produced every year, and packaging accounts for about 36% of plastic production.

Our short film is aimed at everyday consumers who buy plastic-packaged products. It shows what can happen after plastic is thrown away and encourages people to think about more sustainable packaging alternatives.

**What did you build?**
We created a 30-second AI-generated short film that compares plastic packaging with banana-leaf packaging. The film alternates between negative scenes of plastic and positive scenes of banana leaves.

The film follows both materials through different stages: production, products in a supermarket, disposal after use, and their final impact on nature. Plastic can end up in the ocean and harm animals, while banana leaves can biodegrade naturally.


**Link to the live thing (if any):**

https://youtu.be/Y_W9cMousSE?is=wEJwg0T4H8UXx_pR


**How do I run it?**
Open the ComfyUI workflow included in the `hackathon/` folder. Make sure the required models are installed and load the workflow into ComfyUI. The shot list contains the prompts and order of the scenes needed to recreate the film.


**Who did what?**
e divided the work equally and fairly. We worked on many parts of the project together and stayed in close contact through WhatsApp to discuss our ideas, progress and decisions. This made sure that we both contributed equally to the final result.

**Ethical reflection - what are the risks of your tool? Who could it harm?**
The film is AI-generated, so the scenes are not real evidence of what happens in every situation. AI can exaggerate the difference between plastic and sustainable alternatives. We therefore use the film to create awareness, rather than presenting the generated images as scientific facts.
